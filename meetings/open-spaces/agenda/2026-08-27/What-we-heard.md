# What We Heard — Gimbal Labs Season 7 Open Spaces

We started Season 7 by looking back, surfacing tensions, and asking what Gimbal Labs needs to think about over the next six months.

We didn't try to solve everything. We tried to identify the questions that matter.

And there was an important reason for doing this work now:

**We want to get back to building things.**

Gimbal Labs works best when people have the agency to learn, experiment, build, teach and create together. Administration, finances, governance, legal structure and meetings should support that work — not become the work.

So part of starting Season 7 is doing the necessary housekeeping: understand our finances, decide what we're maintaining, clarify responsibilities, establish the minimum structures we need, and make conscious decisions about what we're willing to support.

**Get the foundation good enough, then get out of the way and build.**

A few tensions kept appearing:

**We have more intentions than resources.**  
Gimbal Labs has historically done a lot: Open Spaces, Playground, SPO, dRep, live coding, courses, Catalyst projects, partnerships, hackathons and more. Our money, time, skills and attention are finite. What should we actually commit to?

**Preserve the treasury or invest toward sustainability?**  
Our funds will eventually run out. Do we practice austerity, continue spending normally, or deliberately invest some of the treasury in creating sustainable activities and sources of revenue?

**Clubhouse or corporate entity?**  
Gimbal Labs is an open, friendly place where people meet, learn and build. But without an appropriate legal entity, we may be unable to sign contracts or take on larger opportunities. Can we gain those capabilities without losing the culture we've built?

**Volunteerism or sustainable participation?**  
We value voluntary participation and individual agency. But we don't want participation to require people to spend their life savings or burn themselves out. What work should be paid, and where does that money come from?

**Intentions or commitments?**  
There are many things we'd like Gimbal Labs to do. But every initiative requires some combination of money, time, skills, administration and responsibility. Who is actually willing to commit to each intention?

**Meetings or meaningful spaces?**  
Open Spaces and other meetings create connections and help us understand what people across the network are doing. But meetings take time, and information often disappears afterward. How do we make each space worth the commitment?

**What is Gimbal Labs?**  
A community? A clubhouse? An open-source builder network? An incubator? A business? Infrastructure for people with ideas?

Maybe answering that question again each season is part of what Gimbal Labs is.

These are **tensions, not decisions**.

The goal isn't to spend Season 7 talking about Gimbal Labs.

The goal is to resolve enough of these foundational questions that people know what resources are available, what the guardrails are, what they're responsible for, and where they have room to act.

Then we can spend the balance of the season doing what we came here to do:

**build things, learn things, try things, and help each other make useful things happen.**
