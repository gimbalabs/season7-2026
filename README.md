# Gimbalabs Season 7 — 2026–2027

This repository is the shared organizational record for **Gimbalabs Season 7**.

Gimbalabs provides open, replicable safe spaces to learn, explore, and empower individuals and organizations anywhere so that we can solve meaningful problems.

Season 7 continues that work while experimenting with a more open, GitHub-based way of coordinating Gimbalabs activities, projects, proposals, meetings, and governance.

## What This Repository Is For

This repository collects the organizational work and history of Season 7.

That includes:

* meeting agendas and notes
* Gimbalabs proposals
* governance discussions and decisions
* organizational and legal work
* links to projects taking place across Gimbalabs
* templates and resources used during the season

It is intended to leave a useful public record of not only **what Gimbalabs worked on**, but also **how decisions were made, what tensions surfaced, and what we learned along the way**.

### What belongs here — and what belongs in a project repo?

A useful rule of thumb:

> **Organizational work of Gimbalabs belongs here. Project-specific work belongs in the project's own repository.**

Meetings, Gimbalabs proposals, governance decisions, organizational questions, and Season-wide documentation belong here.

When a project has its own repository, its code, detailed documentation, technical issues, deliverables, and day-to-day work should remain there. Season 7 can link to that work rather than duplicate it.

[The Season 7 GitHub Project](https://github.com/orgs/gimbalabs/projects/7/views/1) provides the cross-repository view that connects these different pieces of work.

## How We Work

Gimbalabs uses ideas and patterns from consent-based and decentralized governance.

Rather than requiring everyone to agree, we look for proposals that are:

> **Good enough for now and safe enough to try.**

Proposals can be discussed, questioned, improved, tried, reviewed, and adapted as we learn.

Open Spaces and other Gimbalabs meetings provide places for people to raise ideas, surface tensions, make proposals, share work, and participate in decisions.

## Season 7 on GitHub

In previous seasons, much of Gimbalabs project coordination and organizational knowledge lived in Notion.

For Season 7, we are experimenting with GitHub as an open organizational workspace.

Different GitHub tools serve different purposes:

* **This repository** holds the organizational record of Season 7.
* **[GitHub Projects](https://github.com/orgs/gimbalabs/projects/7/views/1)** provides a cross-project view of current work and status.
* **[GitHub Issues in this repo](https://github.com/gimbalabs/season7-2026/issues)** track specific work, questions, proposals, and activities.
* **Individual repositories or shared folders** remain the home for projects that have their own code, documentation, issues, and contributors.
* **Discord and meetings** remain important spaces for conversation, coordination, and deliberation.

The goal is not to recreate Notion inside a single GitHub repository. Instead, Season 7 uses GitHub's distributed structure while maintaining a shared organizational record.

## Proposals and Consent

Gimbalabs uses proposals to turn ideas, opportunities, and tensions into concrete experiments. Anyone in the community can bring a proposal forward.

### How to Make a Proposal

1. Have an idea

Identify something you'd like to create, improve, change, or explore with Gimbalabs.

2. Draft your proposal

- Copy the [Gimbalabs Proposal Template](resources/proposal-template.md).
- Edit the document using whatever tools you're comfortable with.
- Publish your proposal somewhere others can access and review it, such as:
  - A GitHub repository (encouraged)
  - HackMD
  - Google Docs or another shared document platform

Your proposal should be accessible to the community and available for feedback and revision throughout the consent process.

3. Submit your proposal to Gimbalabs

Create a new issue in the [Season 7 GitHub Issues](https://github.com/gimbalabs/season7-2026/issues). You can also fork the Season7 repo or create a new branch and connect your PR with the Project Board.

Include:
- A descriptive title
- A brief explanation of your idea
- A link to your public proposal document and/or repository

This issue becomes the tracking point for your proposal. Gimbalabs administrators will add it to the [Season 7 Project Board](https://github.com/orgs/gimbalabs/projects/7), where its progress can be followed.

4. Promote your proposal

Invite the community to participate:

- Come to Open Space and introduce your idea during the call for proposals.
- Share your proposal in [Gimbalabs Discord](https://discord.gg/WWMBExJjHM).
- Schedule time at an upcoming Open Space to present your proposal for the consent process.

5. Present your proposal and participate in the consent process

Present your proposal at Open Space, explain its purpose and intended outcomes, and invite questions and feedback.

Through the consent process, community members can raise concerns, suggest improvements, and help refine the proposal toward a decision.

The goal is not to create a perfect proposal, but to develop something that is:

**Good enough for now. Safe enough to try.**
