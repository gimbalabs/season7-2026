# Gimbalabs Season 7 — 2026–2027

This repository is the shared organizational record for **Gimbalabs Season 7**.

Gimbalabs provides open, replicable safe spaces to learn, explore, and empower individuals and organizations anywhere so that we can solve meaningful problems.

Season 7 continues that work while experimenting with a more open, GitHub-based way of coordinating Gimbalabs activities, projects, proposals, meetings, and governance.

## What This Repository Is For

This repository collects the organizational work and history of Season 7.

That includes:

* meeting agendas and notes
* Gimbalabs proposals
* governance discussions and decisions
* organizational and legal work
* links to projects taking place across Gimbalabs
* templates and resources used during the season

It is intended to leave a useful public record of not only **what Gimbalabs worked on**, but also **how decisions were made, what tensions surfaced, and what we learned along the way**.

### What belongs here — and what belongs in a project repo?

A useful rule of thumb:

> **Organizational work of Gimbalabs belongs here. Project-specific work belongs in the project's own repository.**

Meetings, Gimbalabs proposals, governance decisions, organizational questions, and Season-wide documentation belong here.

When a project has its own repository, its code, detailed documentation, technical issues, deliverables, and day-to-day work should remain there. Season 7 can link to that work rather than duplicate it.

The Season 7 GitHub Project provides the cross-repository view that connects these different pieces of work.

## How We Work

Gimbalabs uses ideas and patterns from consent-based and decentralized governance.

Rather than requiring everyone to agree, we look for proposals that are:

> **Good enough for now and safe enough to try.**

Proposals can be discussed, questioned, improved, tried, reviewed, and adapted as we learn.

Open Spaces and other Gimbalabs meetings provide places for people to raise ideas, surface tensions, make proposals, share work, and participate in decisions.

## Season 7 on GitHub

In previous seasons, much of Gimbalabs project coordination and organizational knowledge lived in Notion.

For Season 7, we are experimenting with GitHub as an open organizational workspace.

Different GitHub tools serve different purposes:

* **This repository** holds the organizational record of Season 7.
* **GitHub Projects** provides a cross-project view of current work and status.
* **GitHub Issues** track specific work, questions, proposals, and activities.
* **Individual repositories** remain the home for projects that have their own code, documentation, issues, and contributors.
* **Discord and meetings** remain important spaces for conversation, coordination, and deliberation.

The goal is not to recreate Notion inside a single GitHub repository. Instead, Season 7 uses GitHub's distributed structure while maintaining a shared organizational record.

### Meetings

`meetings/` contains agendas, notes, decisions, actions, questions, and other records from recurring Gimbalabs meetings.

`meetings/open-spaces/` contains the Season 7 Open Spaces meeting record.

### Proposals

`proposals/` contains proposals concerning Gimbalabs and Season 7.

Gimbalabs proposals build on the proposal and consent practices developed during previous seasons.

### Governance

`governance/` contains organizational governance work that does not naturally belong to an individual project.

This can include decisions, organizational structures, policies, and Season 7 work exploring an appropriate legal structure for Gimbalabs.

### Projects

Gimbalabs projects may live in repositories across the Gimbalabs GitHub organization or elsewhere.

The `projects/` directory provides an index and organizational context rather than duplicating documentation that belongs in those project repositories.

### Resources

`resources/` contains reusable Season 7 resources and documentation.

## Meetings and Organizational Memory

Meeting notes are more than transcripts.

During Season 7 we are experimenting with deliberately capturing useful organizational information such as:

* tensions surfaced
* observations and important moments
* decisions
* actions
* questions to carry forward

This creates a lightweight organizational memory while allowing meetings and conversations to remain fluid.

## Proposals and Consent

Gimbalabs has historically used proposals as a way to turn ideas and tensions into concrete experiments.

A proposal may begin informally through conversation or at Open Spaces, become documented for community review, and evolve through questions, reactions, objections, and adaptation.

Season 7 continues to develop this process while moving proposal documentation and coordination from Notion into GitHub.
