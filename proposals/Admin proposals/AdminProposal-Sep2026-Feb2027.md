# Gimbalabs Admin Operations and Maintenance Budget

**Proposal period:** September 2026–February 2027  
**Duration:** Six months  
**Status:** Draft

## Gimbalabs Primary Driver

> To provide open, replicable safe spaces to learn, to explore, and to empower individuals and organizations anywhere so that we can solve meaningful problems.

## Proposal Driver

### Current Situation

Gimbalabs requires ongoing administrative work and routine operational spending to maintain its spaces, infrastructure, governance processes, accounts, services, and organizational activities.

### Effects

Without a predefined operating budget, routine maintenance expenses may require repeated governance proposals, creating unnecessary administrative overhead and slowing down normal operations.

### Need

Gimbalabs needs a clearly defined six-month operating budget that allows the Admins/Managers to handle routine maintenance and operational costs while keeping spending transparent and accountable.

## Proposal

### 1. Title or Brief Description

**Gimbalabs Admin Operations and Maintenance Budget — 6 Months**

### 2. Background or Rationale

Gimbalabs Admins currently handle ongoing operational responsibilities, including maintaining organizational infrastructure, services, governance spaces, accounts, records, and other routine needs.

The Gimbalabs LLC Agreement also distinguishes routine operational authority from major organizational decisions and allows Managers to execute spending already authorized through an approved proposal or budget.

Rather than requiring individual governance proposals for ordinary maintenance expenses, this proposal establishes a defined six-month operating budget.

### 3. Intended Outcome

For the six-month proposal period:

- Essential Gimbalabs operations and infrastructure remain maintained.
- Open governance spaces continue to be facilitated.
- Gimbalabs keeps up with X posting and other social media.
- The Gimbalabs account on GitHub remains maintained.
- The Gimbalabs website remains maintained.
- Admins can handle ordinary maintenance expenses without submitting a separate proposal for every expense.
- Operational spending remains documented and transparent.
- Admin responsibilities continue to be compensated.
- Expenses outside the approved scope or requiring separate governance approval continue to go through the governance process.
- Additional funding sources and marketing activities are pursued.

### 4. Details of the Proposal

This proposal authorizes two separate budgets for a period of six months.

#### A. Operations and Maintenance Budget

Up to **$5,000 USD total (26,000 ADA)** may be spent during the six-month period on reasonable Gimbalabs operational and maintenance expenses.

This may include:

- Software and subscriptions
- Marketing
- Hosting, domains, and infrastructure
- Registered-agent and organizational costs
- Accounting and routine compliance costs
- Tools required for Gimbalabs activities
- Maintenance of organizational accounts and systems
- Governance and community infrastructure
- Reasonable reimbursements for Gimbalabs operational expenses
- Other ordinary maintenance expenses necessary to keep Gimbalabs operating

This is a **$5,000 total budget for the entire six-month period**, not $5,000 per expense.

Expenses must be related to legitimate Gimbalabs operations and recorded so the community can understand how the budget was used.

**Examples of recent expenses include:**

- Incorporation expenses: $250
- Domain names: $120
- Jitsi may be substituted for Zoom to reduce expenses.

#### B. Admin Compensation

Each Admin will receive **1,500 ADA per month**.

**Current Admins:**

- Harsha Gullapalli — 1,500 ADA/month
- Newman Lanier — 1,500 ADA/month

**Total monthly Admin compensation:** 3,000 ADA  
**Total maximum Admin compensation over six months:** 18,000 ADA

Admin compensation is separate from the **$5,000 / 26,000 ADA** operations and maintenance budget.

### 5. Impact and Benefits

This proposal gives the Admins enough operational authority to maintain Gimbalabs without repeatedly bringing small routine expenses through governance.

At the same time, it creates:

- A clear spending limit
- A defined time period
- Transparent records
- Predictable Admin compensation
- An opportunity for governance to review the arrangement before renewing it

### 6. Evaluation Criteria

At the end of the six-month period, evaluate:

- Whether essential Gimbalabs operations were maintained
- How much of the $5,000 budget was actually used
- What categories the money was spent on
- Whether spending was adequately documented
- Whether the Admin workload justified the compensation
- Whether the budget amount should increase, decrease, or remain the same
- Whether any operational expenses should receive their own dedicated budgets in the future
- Marketing and outreach results
- What funding sources were contacted

### 7. Timeline

**Duration:** Six months from the proposal's effective date.

The proposal expires automatically at the end of the six-month period unless renewed through governance.

A review should occur near the end of the period before any renewal. This proposal, like all proposals, will undergo a monthly review during governance sessions.

### 8. Resources Required

```text
(Admin compensation + Maintenance Cost) / 6 months
= (18,000 ADA + 26,000 ADA) / 6 months
= 44,000 ADA / 6 months
```

**Note:** 1 ADA is approximately $0.20 USD.

### 9. Responsibilities

The Admins are responsible for:

- Facilitating open governance spaces
- Keeping up with X posting and other social media
- Maintaining the Gimbalabs account on GitHub
- Maintaining the Gimbalabs website

### 10. Potential Risks and Mitigation Strategies

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Spending becomes too discretionary. | Spending must relate to Gimbalabs operations and maintenance and must be documented. |
| The $5,000 budget is insufficient. | Additional funding can be requested through a new or amended governance proposal. |
| Long-term compensation continues without review. | This authorization automatically ends after six months unless governance explicitly renews it. |

### 11. Review and Adaptation Plan

A review will occur near the end of the six-month period.

Governance may then:

- Renew the proposal
- Change the maintenance budget
- Change Admin compensation
- Modify the scope of authorized spending
- Replace this proposal with a longer-term operating budget
- Discontinue the arrangement

If circumstances materially change before the six-month review, an amendment may be brought to governance earlier.

If this proposal is approved before Gimbalabs LLC is legally formed, the proposal may subsequently be formally adopted and ratified by Gimbalabs LLC after formation so that it becomes an authorized Company budget under the LLC's governance records.
