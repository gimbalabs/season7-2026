# Gimbalabs as an Andamio Channel Partner: Cardano XP and Coach

**Proposed by:** James Dunseith
**Status:** Draft

**Disclosure:** James works on Andamio. This proposal asks Gimbalabs whether to help bring people to Andamio, so the community should weigh it knowing that.

## Gimbalabs Primary Driver

> To provide open, replicable safe spaces to learn, to explore, and to empower individuals and organizations anywhere so that we can solve meaningful problems.

Gimbalabs funds experiments, not just activities. Every experimental proposal must produce evidence, reusable knowledge, and, where relevant, a testable path toward future income.

## Proposal Driver

### Current Situation

James built two tools that are useful on their own and that both lead people toward Andamio.

- **Cardano XP** connects builders who need feedback with people who want to help Cardano. Contributors review apps, courses and proposals, and earn XP tokens on-chain as proof of their work. It runs on Andamio's project and credential infrastructure, so every XP contributor is also an Andamio user.
- **Coach** is a desktop app for building credentials and offering them to your AI agents. You define what a credential means, write the assignments that prove it, and test which of your agents can do the work. Where no agent can, you need a person who holds that credential, and Andamio is where people find each other by credential. Coach doesn't push anyone there. It shows the path when they need it.

Both repos sit on James's personal GitHub account today. XP is already a Gimbalabs-funded project: Gimbalabs consented to *Cardano Community Feedback Tooling* at Open Space on 2026-04-02, with a 15,000 ADA budget. XP's project holds those funds.

### Effects

Gimbalabs is looking for a path to self-sustainability (season7-2026 issue 9). One possible path is to be a channel partner: a community that runs useful tools and programs, and in doing so brings people and organizations to a platform like Andamio. Gimbalabs hasn't decided whether it wants that role.

### Need

Gimbalabs needs to decide whether to try being a channel partner for Andamio. If it does, it needs a small, bounded experiment that shows whether the role works for Gimbalabs.

## Proposal

### 1. Title or Brief Description

**Gimbalabs as an Andamio Channel Partner: Cardano XP and Coach**

### 2. Background or Rationale

Gimbalabs started with project-based learning and with credentials that show what someone can do. Andamio grew from the same work. A channel partnership would make that link explicit: Gimbalabs runs tools and programs its community wants, and those tools lead people to Andamio when they need it.

The question comes first. XP and Coach are how to test it, not the reason for it. If Gimbalabs says no to the role, it can still take on either tool on its own merits.

Cardano XP is not new to Gimbalabs. The *Cardano Community Feedback Tooling* proposal (https://github.com/gimbalabs/gimbalabs-project-archive/blob/main/projects/active/cardano-community-feedback-tooling/proposal.md) funded it with 15,000 ADA for hosting, sponsored transactions, project admin and the project team. This proposal builds on that one rather than replacing it.

- Cardano XP: https://github.com/workshop-maybe/cardano-xp · https://cardano-xp.vercel.app
- Coach: https://github.com/workshop-maybe/coach-desktop 

### 3. Intended Outcome

- Gimbalabs answers the question: does it want to be a channel partner for Andamio, yes, no or not yet?
- If yes, the XP and Coach repos move to the Gimbalabs GitHub organization, under Gimbalabs stewardship.
- Follow-up work, budgets and releases are left to later proposals.

### 4. Details of the Proposal

#### A. The question

Does Gimbalabs want to try being a channel partner for Andamio?

Whether to pursue this relationship is Gimbalabs' decision. If Gimbalabs wants it, James will work with Gimbalabs and Andamio to shape terms that benefit both, such as revenue share, referral terms or API credits. No terms are set yet, and none are agreed by James alone. Section 12 of the template already allows a project to set its own revenue-sharing arrangement.

#### B. Cardano XP

- XP already runs on Gimbalabs funds and is already hosted on the Gimbalabs Vercel account. The change is stewardship: Gimbalabs takes part in running it, not only funding it.
- The 15,000 ADA is unspent. All of it sits in the treasury of XP's on-chain project, ready for contributor rewards, hosting and fees.
- The feedback proposal's week-12 review is folded into this one.
- If this proposal is approved, the XP repo moves to the Gimbalabs GitHub organization.
- James is the maintainer. Until Gimbalabs sets a maintainer policy, he keeps working on it as he has, and invites others to get involved.
- Pending work is settled first: contributors have accepted commitments waiting for rewards.
- Decide who holds the project's mainnet signing keys after the hand-over.
- A known issue is disclosed up front: the leaderboard falls back to placeholder data because one endpoint call is missing user authentication. It doesn't block contributors.

#### C. Coach

- If this proposal is approved, the Coach repo moves from James's personal account to the Gimbalabs GitHub organization.
- This proposal makes no commitment to a public release of the app.
- James is the maintainer, on the same terms as XP.

### 5. Impact and Benefits

- Gimbalabs gets a first answer on a possible path to self-sustainability, with tools that already exist.
- The community gets stewardship of two working repos whatever the answer on the channel question.
- Andamio adoption, if it comes, comes from tools people chose for their own sake.

### 6. Evaluation Criteria

- **The channel:** how many people or organizations came to Andamio through XP or Coach, and what Gimbalabs received in return.
- **The repos:** both moved to the Gimbalabs organization, and who has joined as a contributor.
- **The decision:** at review, should Gimbalabs continue the role, change its terms or stop?

### 7. Timeline

- Present at Open Space: 2026-10-29
- Settle XP's pending rewards and decide where its keys live: within one month of consent
- Move both repos: within one month of consent
- Review: six months after consent

### 8. Resources Required

- **People:** James as maintainer, plus contributors who join. No team is required to start.
- **XP hosting:** the front end already runs on the Gimbalabs Vercel account. James pays that bill today; it is the Vercel line in the Gimbalabs Infrastructure Costs and Stake Pool proposal.
- **Andamio:** XP needs an Andamio API key, and its rewards run on Andamio's project infrastructure.
- **ADA:** XP's remaining funds from the 15,000 ADA feedback-tooling budget cover hosting, transaction fees and contributor rewards. No new ADA is requested for XP. XP tokens themselves are already minted (100,000 fixed supply).
- **Coach:** no hosting. It runs on each user's computer.
- **Budget:** none requested.

### 9. Responsibilities

- **James:** maintainer of both repos, the hand-over, inviting contributors, and the link to Andamio.
- **Gimbalabs:** a maintainer policy for its repos, in a follow-up proposal. It sets how other maintainers are added.
- **XP contributors:** posting tasks, reviewing contributions and releasing rewards, alongside James.
- **Coach contributors:** improvements and community feedback, alongside James.
- **Admins:** record what the channel role brings in, for the review.

### 10. Potential Risks and Mitigation Strategies

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Gimbalabs becomes a sales arm for one company. | The tools stay useful on their own and open source. The role is reviewed at six months, and Gimbalabs can stop. |
| James is on both sides. | Disclosed at the top. Any terms between Gimbalabs and Andamio are agreed by others on each side, not by James alone. |
| The role costs Gimbalabs effort and brings nothing back. | Section 6 measures what it brings. No terms, no continuation. |
| XP depends on Andamio infrastructure. | That is the point of the channel, and it is documented in the repo. |
| The mainnet signing keys are mishandled during the hand-over. | Decide custody before the move, and never commit keys to a repo. |
| Coach shares its name with Andamio's course-development plugin, also called Coach. | Decide whether to keep the name or rename the app. |

### 11. Review and Adaptation Plan

Review at six months against section 6. Gimbalabs can continue the channel role, change its terms, keep the tools without the role, or stop.

### 12. Licensing and Intellectual Property

- **Cardano XP:** Apache 2.0. Code that came from Andamio's app template keeps its MIT notice in the repo's NOTICE file.
- **Coach:** Apache 2.0. It includes code adapted from Andamio's credential-badges (Apache 2.0) and badges drawn from Andamio's coach plugin (MIT). Both licenses allow this, and the attributions are already recorded in the repo's NOTICE file.
- **Ownership:** both repos are on James's personal account today. XP's funds already come from Gimbalabs. On consent, both repos pass to Gimbalabs.
- **Revenue sharing:** whatever terms come with the channel role are recorded here once agreed.
- **XP tokens:** a reputation token, not a financial instrument. The policy and supply are already on-chain.
