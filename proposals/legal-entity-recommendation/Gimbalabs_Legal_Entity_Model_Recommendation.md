# Why Gimbalabs Should Become a Delaware Statutory Public Benefit LLC

**Meeting brief — August 2026**

## Recommendation

Form Gimbalabs as a **Delaware Statutory Public Benefit LLC**.

The entity should be broad enough to run public-benefit, educational, open-source, grant-funded, and revenue-producing experiments under one legal home.

## 1. The problem we are solving

Gimbalabs has outgrown an informal structure.

Today, trusted individuals may end up personally carrying responsibility for funds, wallets, contracts, domains, software, grants, sponsorships, contributor payments, and other organizational assets.

That becomes less durable as Gimbalabs takes on larger projects and experiments.

The goal is not simply to create a company. The goal is to give Gimbalabs a legal home that can support many kinds of experiments while protecting the people involved and preserving Gimbalabs' public-purpose mission.

## 2. What Gimbalabs needs the entity to do

Gimbalabs needs an entity that can:

- Receive grants, sponsorships, program revenue, and other funding.
- Enter contracts with foundations, companies, partners, and contributors.
- Pay contributors, contractors, maintainers, and service providers.
- Own organizational assets such as domains, software, IP, bank accounts, ADA, and wallets.
- Run both mission-driven and revenue-producing experiments.
- Keep operating even when individual administrators change.
- Reinvest revenue into future Gimbalabs work and experiments.

## 3. Why the Public Benefit LLC fits

### It combines public purpose with commercial flexibility

Gimbalabs can pursue an educational and community benefit while still earning revenue, receiving grants, paying people, owning assets, and operating programs that may become commercially successful.

### It supports experimentation

Gimbalabs does not need one fixed business model.

Some experiments may be grant-funded, some may generate revenue, some may create open-source public goods, and some may later become standalone ventures.

The entity can support all of these.

### It preserves governance flexibility

The LLC agreement can be designed around Gimbalabs' consent-based governance and existing decision practices, while still defining who has legal authority to sign contracts, move funds, and act for the entity.

### It avoids unnecessary corporate complexity today

Gimbalabs is currently solving for sustainability, continuity, and experimentation rather than conventional venture-capital fundraising.

A corporation can be reconsidered later if that changes.

## 4. How to think about the entity

**Gimbalabs itself becomes the legal entity.**

The Public Benefit LLC is not just a wrapper around individual administrators. It is the organization that can own assets, receive money, sign agreements, and continue over time.

**Experiments come and go; Gimbalabs persists.**

A Sustainability Hackathon, Piece of Pie-style program, paid learning program, open-source tool, research project, or future revenue experiment can all sit inside the same institutional home.

## 5. Ownership should be explicit, not assumed

Gimbalabs should not automatically own every project it supports, and creators should not automatically own work commissioned for Gimbalabs.

The ownership model should be decided before meaningful money or support is provided.

Possible ownership models include:

- **Creator-owned:** The builder keeps the IP; Gimbalabs supports the project under agreed terms.
- **Gimbalabs-owned:** Gimbalabs owns work created for its own products, infrastructure, or programs.
- **Shared ownership:** Gimbalabs and named creators intentionally share rights under a written agreement.
- **Spinout:** A successful experiment can move into its own entity, with Gimbalabs' continuing rights defined in advance.

## 6. Standardize the legal paperwork

A simple operating rule:

> When Gimbalabs provides meaningful money, developer time, infrastructure, services, mentorship, or other support, the project should use a standard written agreement.

Suggested reusable templates:

- Creator-Owned Support Agreement
- Gimbalabs-Owned Build Agreement
- Shared Ownership Agreement
- Spinout / Venture Transition Agreement
- Contributor Agreement

This keeps ownership predictable, protects both Gimbalabs and builders, and avoids renegotiating basic legal terms from scratch for every experiment.

## 7. What sustainability looks like under this model

Gimbalabs does not need every experiment to make money.

The organization can operate as a portfolio:

- some work produces public value
- some earns revenue
- some is funded by partners or grants
- some may create long-term IP or spinout value

The sustainability objective is at the **Gimbalabs level**.

Over time, recurring commercial programs, sponsored experiments, grants, services, IP, and other revenue sources should collectively cover the organization's operating needs and fund future experimentation.

## Decision in one sentence

**Form Gimbalabs as a Delaware Statutory Public Benefit LLC so it has a durable legal home for mission-driven and revenue-producing experiments, with clear ownership rules whenever Gimbalabs money or support is involved.**
